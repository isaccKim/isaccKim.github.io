CVPR 2026 Co-first author

MonoSAOD: Monocular 3D Object Detection with Sparsely Annotated Label

Junyoung Jung*, Seokwon Kim*, Jung Uk Kim

IEEE/CVF Conference on Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition (CVPR), 2026

Monocular 3D detection when only a fraction of objects are labeled. Road-Aware Patch Augmentation pastes segmented object patches onto road regions while keeping 3D geometry consistent, and Prototype-Based Filtering keeps pseudo-labels that agree with learned RoI-feature prototypes and have low depth uncertainty.

Radiology: AI 2026 JIF 20.1 · Q1

ARNAI: Artifact Removal Network Based on Autoencoding and Inpainting for Robust Spinal Image Segmentation and Measurement

Sang-Jin Park, Jinyoung Choi, Seokwon Kim, Seungeon Song, Insu Park, Dougho Park, Taeyeon Kim, Youjin Lee, Donghoon Yang, Jae Man Cho, Joong Won Yang, Mansu Kim, Heum Dai Kwon, Hong-Gyu Baek, Dae-Chul Cho, Injung Kim

Radiology: Artificial Intelligence, 8(6):e251134, 2026

Industry–Academic Project with Medical Research Institute, Pohang Stroke and Spine Hospital

Removes metallic implant artifacts from postoperative spine radiographs so that vertebrae can be segmented and spinopelvic parameters measured automatically — without any implant-containing training data. Mean Dice 0.814 → 0.870, and L4–L5 Cobb-angle error reduced by ~70% on the internal test set.

MEDINFO 2025

A New Approach to Perfusion-Weighted Image Analysis: Measurement via Red–Green–Blue Information Inversion

Taeyeon Kim, Youngsoo Kim, Youjin Lee, Injung Kim, Seungeon Song, Seokwon Kim, Jinyoung Choi, Dougho Park

Studies in Health Technology and Informatics (MEDINFO 2025), pp. 896–900

Industry–Academic Project with Medical Research Institute, Pohang Stroke and Spine Hospital

A Python GUI program that measures ROI values directly from RGB-reconstructed perfusion MR images, removing the dependence on vendor-specific source data. Shows high agreement with conventional analysis for CBV and TTP (ICC 0.91 and 0.94).
